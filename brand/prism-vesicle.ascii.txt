PRISM VESICLE — terminal mark (derived from the brand renders)
==================================================================

Ramp: 7-bit ASCII density chars " .:-=+*#%@". No box-drawing or
block glyphs; safe for logs, pipes, and any code page.

COLOUR (optional ANSI layer)
  Plain output stays legible without colour. On truecolour terminals,
  tint by density: %#@ chars toward emerald #10b981 (beam) and
  #34d399 (rim hot spots); =+* toward #047857; .:- toward #151916.
  In the PRIMARY mark the beam row (left, with @) is the brand beam;
  the faint right-hand fan may be tinted through the engine spectrum
  #22d3ee #facc15 #fb923c #f43f5e #e879f9.
  256-colour fallback: 35/34 (emerald deep), 48 (bright), 236 (dim),
  39 220 214 209 207 (spectrum).
